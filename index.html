Notes:

  • Player Behaviour
    • Assumes the player is constantly checking on their team and taps any pokemon that have a help ready, except during sleep session.
    • Assumes a single 8-hour sleep session for the purposes of downtime
  • Energy
    • Team energy raised to 100% after sleep session. If higher than 100%, left alone.
    • No upper cap on energy from skills.
  • Berries
    • Sneaky snacking bug(?) that gives more power than the berries are normally worth is not implemented
    • Assumes a 50% chance when helping of choose Berry instead of Ingredient
  • Ingredients
    • Assumes a 50% chance when helping of choose Berry instead of Ingredient
    • Assumes all ingredients are spent on recipies
    • Uses base ingredient value
  • Skills
    • Assumes 10% chance per help to trigger main skill.
    • If a skill triggers during the 8 hour sleep period, 1 trigger is held as ready and will trigger when the player wakes up.
  • Natures
    • Only Skill Chance, Energy Recovery, Ingredient Chance, and Speed of Help currently have any effect in this tool.
